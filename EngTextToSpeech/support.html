Reader

Getting started

Import a document

Tap Scan, File, or Photos at the top of the Reader to bring in text: scan pages with the camera, pick a PDF or image file, or choose photos from your library. You can also type or paste text directly into the box.

Remove the document

Tap the X next to the document's name to clear it and start over. If it isn't saved to your Library yet, this loses it for good, so you'll be asked to confirm.

Pages

Move between pages

For documents with more than one page, the header above the text shows PAGE X OF Y. Use the arrows to move to the previous or next page.

Remove a page

The trash icon in the page header deletes just the page you're currently viewing — useful for a title page, a footnote, or a scan that came out garbled. It won't be included when you save.

Edit the text

Tap into the text box to fix anything before reading it — a typo the scanner introduced, or a line you'd rather skip.

Playback

Speak, Pause, Resume

The main button starts reading the page aloud. While it's playing, the same button pauses it; tap it again to resume from where you left off. The sentence being read is highlighted as it goes.

Stop

Ends the current pass completely, rather than just pausing it.

Resynthesize audio

If a page's audio ever comes out with a distorted voice, this regenerates it from scratch for just that page — free, and it doesn't cost a coin.

Repeat

Off stops after one read-through. Page repeats the page you're on — handy for practicing pronunciation or memorizing just a few sentences without listening to a whole recording again. All moves on to the next page when one finishes, wrapping back to the first page after the last — handy for listening straight through a whole document.

Save & Settings

Save to Library

Saves the document on screen — including any edits — so you can come back to it later from the Library tab.

Voice Setting

Choose the voice, tone, and reading speed. Changing one plays a short preview automatically so you can hear it before committing.

Coins

Each read costs coins, shown in the top corner. Visit Earn Coins to top up, or watch an ad when you run out.

Library

Opening a saved document

The Library tab lists every document you've saved from the Reader, with a cover thumbnail, a text preview, and the page count or character count plus the date it was saved. Tap any item to reopen it in the Reader.

Deleting a document

Swipe left on an item and tap Delete to remove it from your Library. This only removes the saved copy — it doesn't affect coins or your subscription.

Earn Coins

How coins work

Coins pay for reading. Each read or repeat costs a small number of coins, and your balance is always shown at the top of the Earn Coins screen. Pro subscribers don't spend coins at all — reading is unlimited.

Earning more

Tap "Watch an ad" and watch it to the end to receive coins immediately. If no ad is ready, wait a moment for one to load, or switch to a Pro subscription to remove the need for coins entirely.

Pro

Plans & pricing

Pro is offered as a Monthly ($2.99/month) or Yearly ($29.99/year) subscription. Pick a plan on the Pro screen and tap Subscribe; the price shown for your region is confirmed by the App Store before you're charged.

Manage or cancel

Subscriptions are billed through your Apple ID and renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the period ends. Manage or cancel anytime from Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions on your device.

Restore purchases

Already subscribed on another device? Open the Pro screen and tap Restore Purchases to bring your subscription back on this one.
