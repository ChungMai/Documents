Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 7, 2026

English Text To Speech is designed to read imported text, images, and PDF documents on your device.

Data We Process

The app may process text, images, and PDF documents that you import so it can extract text and read it aloud. This processing is intended to happen on device.

Purchases

Subscription purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment card details.

Advertising

The app shows optional rewarded video ads, served through Google AdMob, that let you earn extra coins for reading. Pro subscribers never see ads.

To deliver and measure these ads, Google AdMob and its partners may collect device identifiers, approximate location (from IP address), and app usage data, as described in Google's Privacy Policy. Apple's App Tracking Transparency setting on your device controls whether ads are personalized; if tracking is not authorized, ads are served without cross-app tracking.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Ad-serving data described above is shared with Google AdMob solely to display and measure rewarded ads. If the app uses Apple services such as StoreKit, those services are governed by Apple's privacy practices.

Data Retention

Documents and extracted reading content may be stored locally on your device as part of the app library. You can remove imported content from the app or delete the app to remove local app data from your device.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact maihuuchung@gmail.com.
