Learn the words you hear

From the moment you meet a new word to the day you've mastered it, everything happens in one place.

1 · Capture words and sentences as you read

  • Tap a word, or drag across several to save a whole phrase or sentence.
  • Translate a full page into 14+ languages — your choice is remembered.
  • Organized for you. Lists are grouped by day; swipe to rename or delete.

2 · Know each word properly

  • Meaning in your language plus an example sentence.
  • Phonetic spelling — IPA for English, Pinyin with tone marks for Chinese.
  • Hear every word one at a time, or tap Play All to listen to the whole list.
  • Import and export. Bring in your own lists, or export a list to a PDF with terms, phonetics and meanings — ready to print.

3 · Review with spaced repetition — by ear

  • Daily Review pools every due word from every list, using the proven SM-2 spaced-repetition algorithm.
  • Listen-and-type quiz. A word plays once, you type what you hear — training your ears and spelling together.
  • Hint, Play again and Skip keep it friendly; missed words come back later in the same session.
  • Customize your session — choose how many new and due words to practice.
  • Review any single list as a quick random-order quiz.

4 · Stay motivated and see real progress

  • Streak tracks your current and longest run of daily reviews.
  • Words mastered — three correct reviews in a row — with a clear SM-2 stage chart: New, Learning, Young, Mastered.
  • Home Screen widget shows your streak and words due today; one tap jumps straight into Daily Review.
