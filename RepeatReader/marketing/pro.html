Good to know

Questions people ask

Can I switch from Monthly to Yearly?

Yes, right inside the app. Open the Coins & Pro tab and tap Upgrade to Yearly. The App Store shows the price and any credit for unused time before you are charged.

How do I cancel?

Open Settings, then your Apple Account, then Subscriptions. You keep Pro until the end of the period you already paid for.

I subscribed on another device. How do I get Pro back?

Tap Restore Purchases on the Coins & Pro tab. Pro follows your Apple Account, so there is nothing to sign in to.

Why is audio export only on Yearly?

Exporting generates long audio files, so it is part of the Yearly plan. Monthly includes everything else in Pro.

Does my text leave my iPhone?

No. Voice, text recognition and translation all run on your device, on every plan.
