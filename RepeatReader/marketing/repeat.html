The signature feature

Repeat — language immersion other readers don't offer.

Listening comprehension only improves with repeated exposure. One tap on Repeat turns any page into a loop for your ears.

  • Off · Page · All. Loop the page you're on, or move through the entire document and wrap back to the start.
  • Sentence-by-sentence highlighting lets you read along while you listen — perfect for shadowing practice.
  • Keeps playing in the background and recovers cleanly when you come back.
  • Show the original page image or clean text — whichever helps you focus.

How Repeat works

Three modes, one tap

Off

One read-through

Reads the page once and stops. Great for a first pass.

Page

Loop this page

Plays the page you're on again and again — ideal for shadowing, pronunciation and tough passages.

All

Loop the document

Moves on to the next page when one finishes, then wraps back to the first page after the last.
