Private by design

Everything happens on your iPhone.

Your documents, audio and word lists are yours. There's no sign-in and no server that sees what you read.

On-device voice

Speech is synthesized locally by a neural voice model.

On-device OCR

Text from PDFs and photos is recognized right on your phone.

On-device translation

Meanings are looked up locally — no text sent to a server.

No account

Open the app and start. Back up and restore your data whenever you like.
