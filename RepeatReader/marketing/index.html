Why Repeat Reader

A reading library, an immersion player, and a vocabulary trainer — in one app.

Most text-to-speech apps read a page once and stop. Repeat Reader is built around what language learners really do: listen again and again, look up what they don't know, and review it until it sticks.

🎧

Listen to whatever you want

No audiobook to buy and no narrator you're stuck with. Import a PDF, pick photos of a page, or paste any text — a novel, a news article, your own notes.

🔁

Repeat it until it sinks in

Turn on Repeat to hear a page again and again — or the whole document in a loop — with the sentence being spoken highlighted as you follow along.

🧠

Learn what you hear

Tap a word or drag across a phrase, see its translation and pronunciation, save it, then review it with spaced repetition and listen-and-type quizzes.
