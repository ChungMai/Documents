Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 7, 2026

Repeat Reader: Listen & Vocab reads imported text, images, and PDF documents aloud on your device in English or Chinese, with a Repeat mode that plays a passage again and again so you can immerse your ear in the language — listening practice being one of the hardest language skills to build, and one that only comes from hearing enough of it. It also helps you learn vocabulary from what you read — by translating words, phrases, and sentences you select, and letting you save and review them.

Data We Process

The app may process text, images, and PDF documents that you import so it can extract text and read it aloud. This processing is intended to happen on device.

Vocabulary & Translation

When you tap a word or select a phrase or sentence to look up its meaning, that text is translated using Google ML Kit Translate. Translation runs on device once the language model is downloaded; the initial model download requires a network connection, as described in Google's Privacy Policy. Words and phrases you save, along with your review progress, are stored locally on your device.

Purchases

Subscription purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment card details.

Advertising

The app shows optional rewarded video ads, served through Google AdMob, that let you earn extra coins for reading. Pro subscribers never see ads.

To deliver and measure these ads, Google AdMob and its partners may collect device identifiers, approximate location (from IP address), and app usage data, as described in Google's Privacy Policy. Apple's App Tracking Transparency setting on your device controls whether ads are personalized; if tracking is not authorized, ads are served without cross-app tracking.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Ad-serving data described above is shared with Google AdMob solely to display and measure rewarded ads. Text you select for translation is shared with Google ML Kit Translate solely to return its meaning. If the app uses Apple services such as StoreKit, those services are governed by Apple's privacy practices.

Data Retention

Documents, extracted reading content, saved vocabulary, and review progress may be stored locally on your device as part of the app library. You can remove imported content or saved words from the app, or delete the app to remove local app data from your device.
